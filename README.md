    About Me
  </h3>

  I build **Android** applications with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**, and train **machine learning** models with **Python**. My goal is to bring the two together: on-device intelligence inside real mobile apps.

  - 📱 I develop **native Android apps** with **Kotlin** — **Jetpack Compose**, **Coroutines & Flow**, **Material 3** and **Clean Architecture**.
  - 🎨 I design my own **UI motion**: custom **Compose animations**, canvas drawing and loading states.
  - 🤖 My primary focus is **Machine Learning & AI architectures**, with a strong interest in **model training** and fine-tuning.
  - 👁️ I work on **Computer Vision** — object detection with **YOLO**, image processing with **OpenCV**.
  - 🐳 I use **Docker** for containerization and reproducible training environments.
  - 🌱 Currently going deeper into **Compose internals** and **on-device inference**.
